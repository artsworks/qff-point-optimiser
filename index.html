Gain = what the trip is worth − cash you pay − points used × your ¢/pt value. It's the dollar benefit compared with just buying Economy and keeping your points. Higher is better.

Rules of thumb

  1. Business Classic Reward seats for everyone on good dates? Usually the strongest, simplest choice — Business is confirmed.
  2. No Business reward seats? Look at paid Economy + upgrade — but only on fare classes G K L M S V (78,500 pts pp per flight, Zone 6) or B H Y (43,100). Sale fares in other classes (e.g. E N O Q) can't be upgraded.
  3. The upgrade route mostly buys you points, not Business. It saves points vs a Business Reward but costs more cash. The tool shows the price you're effectively paying per point saved.
  4. Upgrades clear late and a Business seat for sale doesn't mean an upgrade will clear. If it fails you fly Economy and keep your points.
  5. Only one Business seat? Don't split up — try ±1–3 days, other Japan airports, or Business one way + Economy the other.
  6. Rewards open ~353 days out and more seats can appear in batches closer to departure. Search outbound and return separately.
Methodology, accuracy & sources
  • Points come from Qantas' tables for bookings from 5 Aug 2025, per person per one-way trip, by zone (one-way miles). The tool estimates miles from the route you enter (great-circle, summed over connections) — Qantas' own figure can differ slightly, so journeys near a zone edge are flagged; you can set the zone manually.
  • Partner table (e.g. Japan Airlines) is priced higher than Qantas' own flights.
  • ¢/pt = (cash value − cash you pay) ÷ points. For upgrades it's shown if the upgrade clears.
  • Upgrade value = P × Business + (1 − P) × Economy. Points count as spent only with probability P (Qantas only deducts them when the upgrade confirms).
  • Break-even odds = the upgrade probability at which the upgrade route's Gain equals the Business Reward's Gain.
  • Upgrading an Economy Classic Reward (83,600 pp/flight) is never modelled: 72,400 + 2×83,600 = 239,600 pp return, more than a Business Reward (196,800).
  • Holiday and season dates for 2027 are from the VIC government calendar and approximate Japanese holidays/seasons — double-check before booking.
