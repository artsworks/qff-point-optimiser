Decision guide

    Methodology & sources

    • CPP = (comparable cash fare − taxes/fees) ÷ QFF points used, in cents.
    • Upgrade EV = P·Business + (1−P)·Economy − Economy cash paid. Points count as spent only if the upgrade confirms.
    • Breakeven P* solves upgrade-EV (incl. leftover-point value) = Business-Reward value.
    • Zone 6 Classic Rewards pp return: Econ 72,400 · PremEcon 147,600 · Business 196,800 · First 295,400. Bookable ~353 days ahead, capacity-controlled, released in batches.
    • Upgrade: 78,500 × 2 pax × 2 sectors = 314,000; Business Reward for 2 = 393,600; saving = 79,600 pts.
